Welcome

Hello, and welcome to Digitify

Digitify keeps two things in one app: a Library for your books, comics, manga, music, videos, pictures, movies and series, with its own readers and players, and Arcade, a launcher for all your games, from your PC stores and your emulators.

Yours, and only yours

Everything lives on your own computer. No account, no cloud, no tracking: Digitify goes online only when you ask it to, to bring something in from a link or to look for covers.

About this manual

It lives in Library › Books like any book: read it, mark it, or delete it. It updates itself when Digitify brings a new one. Deleted it? Books › Add › Digitify Manual brings it back.

Lost? Press Ctrl + K anywhere and type what you're looking for: a page, a setting, a game, a song.

Chapter 1

Getting started

The bar across the top takes you everywhere. Each page has its own Back button, top left, to return where you were.

Home, Library, Arcade

Home shows what to continue and what's new. Library holds your media, category by category. Arcade holds your games.

Search

Type in the search box (Ctrl + F) to find media and games together on one screen, both or one at a time.

Music, always at hand

The music pill plays your library's music or Digitify's own menu music; once playing it becomes a small player. Click it to open Now playing.

Big Picture

The controller button turns Digitify into a full-screen TV interface for a controller. See chapter 7.

Downloads and imports

The download button shows what's coming in from links and files, and the history of everything that came in, with retry for what failed.

Your profile, and Settings

Your picture opens your profile (chapter 8). The gear opens Settings (Ctrl + ,), sorted in tabs.

Adding your media

Open a category (say Library › Books) and press Add: pick files or whole folders. Each category takes only its own kind of media, so nothing lands in the wrong place. Choose to keep files where they are or copy them into the library folder. Covers, titles, authors and more are read from the files; the same file twice is recognized and skipped. You can also drop files onto the window.

Watched folders

Point Digitify at a folder in Settings › Library and whatever lands in it later is added by itself.

Chapter 2

Home

Home greets you with what matters now: where you left off, what's new, your games and how you're doing.

Continue and Up next

What you were watching, reading or playing, ready to resume; and what comes next in a series. When Digitify opens it offers to continue where you left off.

Rows of your library

Recently added, favorites, "Because you liked" (from your own library), and your Arcade games: recently played and a game of the day.

Make Home yours

Choose which sections show and put them in your order. A photo can even be Home's background.

Statistics

Time spent reading, watching, listening and playing, the last two weeks day by day, monthly goals, your most played artists and games.

Streaks and the calendar

Every day with some activity keeps your streak going. The activity calendar shows your whole year at a glance.

Achievements

They arrive by themselves as you read, watch, listen and play, each with its levels. See them all on their own screen.

Your week, your year

This week, above the statistics, sums up your last seven days; Your week shows them in full and saves them as a picture. Your year looks back on the year: hours, what you finished, best month and day, longest streak, top games, items and artists.

Everything view

Turn on Settings › Library › Show Everything for one view of your whole library at once.

Chapter 3

Library: your collection

Pictures, Books, Comics, Manga, Music, Videos, and Movies & Series: each category in its own place. Reorder or hide categories in Settings.

Media, Collections, Favorites

Each category has three tabs: all its media, its collections (folders of your own; Music has playlists) and your favorites.

Five ways to look

Grid, Shelf (wood, glass or metal), List, Detailed and a 3D Carousel. The Size slider makes covers bigger or smaller.

Sort and filter

By title, date added, year, creator, duration, or your own order: drag covers into place. Filter by Digital or Online. Surprise me opens something you haven't finished.

Select several

Press Select, tick items (Ctrl + A for all) and edit them together: cover, cover shape, favorite, move into a collection, add to a playlist.

Music

Tracks, Albums, Artists and Playlists, each a tab. A music video added to Music comes in as its sound (an MP3), with a frame as its cover.

Videos and Movies & Series

A series keeps its episodes together, by season. Movies & Series › Add › From Videos turns videos you have into movies or episodes.

Books, Comics and Manga

Ebooks (EPUB and more), PDFs, and comics or manga as archives (CBZ, CBR, CB7, CBT) or folders of images. No file? Add › Book from images (or Comic, or Manga) makes one from pictures you put in order.

Discs

Play and rip audio CDs, play and import home-made DVDs and Blu-rays. Copy-protected discs are recognized and left alone.

Chapter 3, continued

Items, collections and pictures

An item's page

Open anything to see its cover big, its details and its Play or Read button. Give it your stars and notes, change its cover or its shape, mark it as a favorite, or move it into a collection with Organize › Move. The arrows at the top step to the previous and next item of the list you came from, going round at the ends.

Collections

Folders of your own inside a category. A collection opens in a window over the category, with Rename and Delete; deleting it never deletes its items.

Playlists

Music's collections. Play one in order or shuffled; add songs from their menu or by selecting several.

Pictures

Photos stay in their albums (from their folders), shown whole in justified rows, with their EXIF details. An album can take any photo as its cover.

The image viewer

Zoom, slideshow, photo info, turn and mirror. Step through an album with the arrows.

Ratings and notes

Your own stars and a note on anything: what you thought, where you left off, who borrowed it.

Open on

Items that came from a website keep a button to open them there.

Library check

Finds files that went missing (an unplugged drive) and duplicates. In Settings › Library.

Chapter 4

From links, and downloads

Music and Videos each have Add › From a link. Paste a link and choose what to bring in: downloaded to keep and play offline, or saved online to play on the site.

Many sites

YouTube, YouTube Music, SoundCloud, Bandcamp, Twitch, Dailymotion and many more, through yt-dlp, which comes with Digitify.

Video qualityMKV or MP4MP3, M4A or original sound

As sound, or as video

From Music's menu a link comes in as sound; from Videos' menu, as video. Downloaded items are plain files, like your own.

Playlists and channels

Listed 25 at a time: pick a section, and come back with the same link for the next. Videos go into a collection and songs into a playlist named after it, which later sections join.

Saved online

An online item plays in the site's own player, and can be downloaded later from its page. Spotify links are always online (their music is protected).

Downloads and imports

The title bar's download button: progress and cancel for what's coming in, and the history of what came in, from links and files, with retry. Clear it any time.

Keeping yt-dlp fresh

Sites change; Update yt-dlp in Settings › Library › Online services fetches the newest one.

Digitify only downloads what you ask for, and never works around copy protection.

Chapter 5

Players and readers

The video player

Subtitles, audio tracks, chapters and bookmarks; films and series resume where you stopped, and next and previous follow the list you came from. Press Back and the video keeps playing in a corner while you browse: drag it to any corner, click it to make it big again.

Video keys

Space play or pause · ← → 5 s · Shift + arrows 30 s · ↑ ↓ volume · M mute · C subtitles · F full screen

Music

A queue, playlists, shuffle and repeat, a mini player in the title bar and a Now playing page. Media keys and the system's media controls (with the cover) work too.

Menu music

Digitify's own calm theme, or your music, playing quietly while you browse. Turn it on or off from the music pill.

Sleep timer

Stops the music after a while, for falling asleep to an album.

The readers

Ebooks with your choice of text size, PDFs, and comics and manga with page-turn effects and right-to-left reading for manga. Bookmarks with B, full screen with F, and a night filter that warms the screen for reading late.

Discs

A disc in the drive shows the disc button in the title bar: play it, rip a CD into Music, or import a home-made DVD.

Chapter 6

Arcade: your games

Arcade finds the games already on your computer and starts each one the way it always starts, with its own settings, counting the time you play.

PC stores

Steam, Epic, Xbox, GOG, Ubisoft, EA, Battle.net, Rockstar, Riot, Amazon and itch.io; Heroic and Lutris on Linux. Minecraft (in Xbox's view): Java opens its launcher; Bedrock, Prism and MultiMC instances start directly.

Emulators

Dolphin, Project64, RMG, DuckStation, PCSX2, RPCS3, PPSSPP, melonDS, DeSmuME, Azahar, Cemu, Ryujinx, xemu, Xenia, Mesen, FCEUX, Nestopia, Snes9x, ZSNES, mGBA, Kega Fusion, Flycast, nullDC, ares, and RetroArch's playlists. No games, BIOS or emulators come with Digitify: it uses the ones you have.

Emulators & folders

Four tabs: Launchers, Emulators (point to a portable one, or set one aside with Don't use), Consoles (which emulator plays each) and Folders & covers. Any other button closes it.

Look for games

In Emulators & folders › Folders & covers, beside the folders it searches. Arcade looks by itself the first time.

Any game by its program

Add a game… takes any game's program; it shows in the Other PC view and starts by itself.

Views

All games (first; switch it off in Settings), PC games, ROM games, each store, and each maker's consoles in one menu. Reorder or hide views in Settings › Arcade.

Sorts

Title, recently played, most played, recently added, your rating, your own order, and Console: each console and launcher under its own heading.

Boxes the right shape

Every console's games have its real box shape; a cover of another shape (another region's) shows whole, never cut.

Chapter 6, continued

Covers, collections and play

A game's panel

Click a game for its panel: Play, time played (add time played elsewhere; Steam's hours, in Launchers, brings in Steam's own), your review, Keywords of your own (the search and dynamic collections find the game by them) and Plays in, to pick the emulator for that game alone. Mark as completed gives its box a green seal. Collections puts it in yours; Cover finds another cover or takes a picture of yours.

Find covers

Every cover found to choose from. Not the right one? Type any words of the name (no need to be exact) and search again.

Collections

Collections, beside Emulators & folders, opens your collections, with a Back to all games. Favorites and Completed are always first.

Dynamic collections

They fill themselves: words in the name or a game's keywords (or not), consoles and launchers, played or not, completed or not, favorites, your rating.

More covers

Download LaunchBox's database once in Settings › Arcade: it knows other names too (Animal Forest is Doubutsu no Mori). A SteamGridDB key adds even more.

While a game runs

Digitify stays open and silences its music, hides until the game ends, or closes: your choice in Settings › Arcade.

Badges

Each box's tag shows its console's logo and name, the logo alone, or nothing: Settings › Arcade › Games' badges.

Select several

Favorite, completed, keywords for all of them, covers, add to a collection, or remove from Digitify (files and installs stay). Surprise me opens a game you haven't completed.

Chapter 8

Your profile

Your own page in Digitify, kept on this computer with no account. Open it from your picture in the title bar.

Edit profile

Your name, a line about you, your picture and a banner (a picture of yours, or a cover from your games and library). Adjust moves and zooms each; the frame is a circle, rounded square or hexagon, with a rainbow, accent, gold or no ring.

What it shows

How much your library holds and in which categories, your games and the hours you've played, how many you've completed, this year's time reading, watching, listening and playing, your best streak, your most played and completed games, and your achievements.

Share as image

Turns your profile into one picture: your banner, picture and name, your big numbers, your most played games and your year so far, signed "Made with Digitify". Save PNG or Copy it, and send it to anyone: it's just a picture, with no account and no link.

Private by design

Nothing about your profile leaves your computer unless you send its picture yourself.

Chapter 9

Make it yours

Four styles

In Settings › General › Appearance, each in light or dark:

Breeze: bright and glossy
Cinematic: dark glass, amber
Frutiger Aero: sky and glass
Minimal: calm and flat

Light or dark

Dark, light, or following your system. Breeze adds palettes: Sky, Sunset, Midnight and High contrast.

Colors

Pick any accent color, or give each section its own.

Performance mode

For slower computers (not the light theme): no blur, no heavy shadows, no animations. Digitify turns it on by itself when your computer struggles, and tells you.

Sounds and motion

Soft interface sounds, and animations you can reduce or turn off.

Around your desktop

Start with your computer, open maximized or at a regular size, keep an icon by the clock (and keep running there quietly when closed), and only one Digitify open at a time.

Keys of your own

Change any shortcut in Settings › Keyboard.

Chapter 11

Safe, private and sound

Backups

Automatic backups of your library, daily, weekly or monthly, to another drive: Settings › Library.

Library check

Finds missing files and duplicates, and helps you put things right. Nothing is ever deleted without asking you first.

Offline first

Digitify works without the internet. It goes online only to bring in links, update yt-dlp, and look for covers when you ask.

No accounts, no tracking

No sign-in, no ads, no telemetry. Optional keys for some services are yours, kept on your computer (Settings › Library › Online services).

Achievements notices

A small notice appears when you reach a new achievement; see them all on their screen.

This manual

Deleted it? Books › Add › Digitify Manual brings it back. It always describes the Digitify you have.

A game doesn't start?

Check that its emulator is found in Arcade › Emulators & folders, or point Arcade to it there. A game set to play in a particular emulator needs that one installed; in its panel, Plays in's first choice goes back to its console's emulator. Missing covers? Find missing covers in Folders & covers, or Find covers in the game's panel.

Something didn't come in?

The download button's history says why, and has Retry.

Thank you for using Digitify. Enjoy your collection, and your games. Digitify is free and open source (MIT); new versions are on its GitHub page (Settings › About).
